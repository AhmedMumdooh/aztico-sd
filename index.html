Shipping & Logistics

We offer end-to-end shipping and logistics services, including customs clearance, storage, and direct delivery. Our strong presence in Sudan and the UAE, along with partnerships with regional and international shipping lines and local land transport providers, ensures reliable support for our exporting clients.

Agriculture Exports

With our extensive experience in agricultural commodity markets — including grains, gum arabic, and animal feed — we guarantee the export of high-quality products that meet agreed specifications and standards. This is achieved by carefully selecting the most suitable sourcing channels, optimizing transportation to shipping ports, and efficiently managing storage, processing, and stuffing operations.

Shipping Agent

Acting as a trusted shipping agent, we handle all port operations, documentation, and vessel coordination on behalf of shipowners and charterers. Our team ensures fast turnaround, operational efficiency, and compliance with maritime regulations.

Supplier

We specialize in supplying the highest-quality furniture and building materials to the local market, including porcelain, lighting systems, high- and low-voltage switches, MCBs, MCCBs, low-voltage cables, plumbing materials, electrical accessories, and electronics. We also ensure their availability and delivery to our customers through our sales outlets and warehouses located in Khartoum and Port Sudan.
